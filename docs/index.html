Your cursor follows your eyes.

With several monitors, you look at another screen and the cursor is still stuck on the old one. Glance watches your webcam, works out which monitor you are looking at, and moves the cursor there. No eye-tracking hardware needed.

Try the live demo Install

How it works

Everything runs locally on your computer. The camera image never leaves it.

01 · See

Track your face

MediaPipe Face Landmarker turns every webcam frame into head rotation, iris position and eye-direction features.

02 · Learn

Calibrate once

Look at five dots on each monitor for about 30 seconds. Glance learns what looking at each screen means for you.

03 · Move

Jump the cursor

When your gaze rests on another monitor, the cursor jumps back to where you last left it there.

Manual control always wins

Glance never fights your trackpad or mouse.

  • Hands on, gaze offNo jumps while you move, click, drag or scroll, and for a short grace period after.
  • Your choice sticksMove the cursor to one monitor while reading another, and Glance leaves it there until your gaze moves somewhere new.
  • No jitterBrief glances are ignored. Your gaze has to rest on a monitor before the cursor jumps.
  • Typing is safeNo jumps while you type. Pause everything at any time with Ctrl+Alt+G.

Install

One command. It installs everything Glance needs, then walks you through setup in about a minute.

curl -fsSL https://sorena-paydar.github.io/Glance/install.sh | sh
irm https://sorena-paydar.github.io/Glance/install.ps1 | iex

Paste it into Terminal (macOS), your shell (Linux) or PowerShell (Windows).

Setup guides you through

Permissions, camera, calibration

On macOS it opens the right System Settings pages for Camera, Accessibility and Input Monitoring, then you follow a red dot across your monitors.

Every day after

Just run glance

Glance sits in your menu bar. Pause with Ctrl+Alt+G. Moved your monitors? It notices and recalibrates.

Uninstall

One command too

uv tool uninstall glance removes it completely.

Try it in your browser

The demo uses the same face features and switching rules as the app, right in this page. Browsers can't move your real cursor, so it shows where Glance would move it.
